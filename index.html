Privacy Policy – MetaLens

Last updated: November 1, 2026 • Application: MetaLens • Developer: Saruga
Global Privacy by Design: MetaLens was built to fully protect your personal data anywhere in the world. All processing of images, videos, audio files, documents, and metadata analysis occurs 100% locally on your own device. Your personal files are never sent to, stored on, or processed by external servers or the cloud.

1. Information Accessed and Purpose of Use

1.1 Local Files and Media

  • What is accessed: Files actively selected by you through the operating system's native file picker (Android Photo Picker or Storage Access Framework).
  • Purpose: Reading, detailed technical inspection, and removal/sanitization of sensitive metadata (including EXIF tags, GPS location coordinates, timestamps, camera models, and software parameters).
  • Storage: All generated or edited files are saved strictly to your device's local storage, with no copies retained by the application.

1.2 Operating System Permissions

The application operates under the principle of least privilege: it requests only the permissions essential to open the files you select and save the sanitized versions to local memory.

3. International Privacy Compliance

MetaLens adheres to global data governance and privacy standards, complying with the requirements of major international regulations:

  • Brazil (LGPD - Law No. 13,709/2018): Guaranteed rights of transparency, non-discrimination, and free access regarding your data and identifiers.
  • European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR): Strictly local processing ensures compliance with the principle of data minimization (Article 5). For advertising, identifier processing is based on consent or regulatory legitimate interest.
  • United States (CCPA / CPRA - California and state privacy laws): We do not sell, monetize, or share files or sensitive personal data with data brokers. Users maintain full rights to deletion and the ability to opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Other Global Jurisdictions: Users in any country benefit from the same level of protection and direct control over the physical storage of their own devices.

4. Consent Management and User Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full control over your information:

  • Resetting Advertising ID: Access your Android privacy settings to reset or delete your Advertising Identifier.
  • Revoking Permissions: Storage permissions can be revoked at any time directly through your system's application settings.
  • Data Deletion: Because no user files or activity history are transmitted to our servers, uninstalling the application permanently removes all local preferences and configurations.

5. Children’s Privacy

MetaLens does not knowingly collect identifiable data from children or minors, in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and related international regulations.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect technical enhancements or evolving global regulatory requirements. The current effective revision date will always appear at the top of this page.
